### 用户研究 / customer-research

版本 2.0.2 · [固定源码](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/customer-research/SKILL.md)

- **适用任务**：想知道用户为什么购买、流失、犹豫或选择替代方案。
- **准备输入**：访谈、问卷、评论、客服记录，或需要调查的问题。

- **典型产物**：研究主题、用户原话、画像、置信度与待验证问题。
- **本地验收提示**：保留来源和用户分组；不把模拟访谈当作真实访谈。
- **本地协作建议**：[product-marketing](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/product-marketing/SKILL.md) → [copywriting](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/copywriting/SKILL.md) → [cro](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/5b2c0007766c6a1cf1d53fd8fc73e979e0821022/skills/cro/SKILL.md)
